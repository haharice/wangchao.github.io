01Research

Analysis at the interface of fluid motion and nonlinear PDEs.

Equations of fluid motion.

My research concerns the well-posedness, stability, and limiting behavior of fluid equations, particularly where boundaries and interfaces play a central role.

Navier–Stokes & Euler equations

Compressible and incompressible flows, inviscid limits, and stability of shear flows.

Boundary layers

Prandtl equations, Gevrey regularity, and the interaction of vortices with boundaries.

Water waves & free boundaries

Capillary-gravity waves, surface tension, corner domains, and contact angles.

Hydrostatic approximation

Fluid and magnetohydrodynamic equations in thin domains.
